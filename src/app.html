How to play

Each side starts with 14 marbles. Push six of your opponent's off the board and you win.

Moving

  • Move 1, 2 or 3 of your own marbles per turn, and they must sit in a straight line.
  • In-line: the column moves along its own axis, like a train.
  • Broadside: a line of 2 or 3 steps sideways — every target space must be empty.

Pushing (sumito)

  • Only an in-line move can push, and only when you have more marbles in the line than your opponent: 2 v 1, 3 v 1, 3 v 2.
  • The space directly behind the pushed group must be empty — or off the board, which removes that marble for good.
  • You can never push your own marbles, and never 3 against 3.
2 v 1 with a gap behind — legal push.
2 v 2 — blocked, no move.

Controls

  • Click a marble to select it; click a neighbour in line to add it (up to three). Click a selected end marble again to drop it.
  • Click a green arrow to move. Copper means a push, red means a marble goes off.
  • Or just drag a selected marble in the direction you want.
  • Keys W E A D Z X move in the six directions; Esc clears.
